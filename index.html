👨‍🎓 Biography

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at Guizhou Normal University. My interests lie in computer graphics, with a focus on real-time rendering and neural rendering. I am currently a rendering intern at Tencent's Morefun Studio, working on neural material representation and GPU inference for game engines.

I enjoy building rendering systems from the ground up — from a from-scratch software rasterizer to a cross-platform game engine (Sakuray Engine) — and understanding how classical and neural approaches trade off in interactive settings.
